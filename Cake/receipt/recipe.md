# Vanilla Cake Recipe

## Description
A classic, moist vanilla cake perfect for any occasion. Simple ingredients, timeless flavor.

## Yield
- 2 round cake layers (8-inch) or 1 layer cake
- Serves 8-12

## Preparation Time
- Prep: 20 minutes
- Bake: 25-30 minutes
- Cool: 1 hour
- **Total: ~1 hour 45 minutes**

---

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| All-purpose flour | 2 ½ cups (315g) |
| Baking powder | 2 ½ tsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |

### Wet Ingredients
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 1 cup (226g) |
| Granulated sugar | 1 ¾ cups (350g) |
| Large eggs, room temperature | 3 |
| Pure vanilla extract | 2 tsp |
| Whole milk, room temperature | 1 cup (240ml) |

### Optional: Vanilla Buttercream Frosting
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 1 cup (226g) |
| Powdered sugar | 3-4 cups (360-480g) |
| Heavy cream | 2-3 tbsp |
| Pure vanilla extract | 2 tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp |

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Grease and flour two 8-inch round cake pans, or line with parchment paper.
3. Set oven rack to the middle position.

### Step 2: Mix Dry Ingredients
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together:
   - Flour
   - Baking powder
   - Salt
2. Set aside.

### Step 3: Cream Butter and Sugar
1. In a large bowl, beat softened butter and sugar together on medium-high speed for **3-4 minutes**, until light and fluffy.
2. Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed.

### Step 4: Add Eggs and Vanilla
1. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
2. Mix in vanilla extract.
3. Scrape down the bowl again.

### Step 5: Combine Wet and Dry
1. Reduce mixer speed to low.
2. Add the dry ingredients in **three parts**, alternating with the milk in **two parts**, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.
   - Add ⅓ of dry ingredients → mix
   - Add ½ of the milk → mix
   - Add ⅓ of dry ingredients → mix
   - Add remaining milk → mix
   - Add remaining dry ingredients → mix
3. Mix just until combined — **do not overmix**.

### Step 6: Bake
1. Divide batter evenly between the prepared pans.
2. Bake for **25-30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
3. Do not open the oven door during the first 20 minutes of baking.

### Step 7: Cool
1. Let cakes cool in pans for **10 minutes**.
2. Remove from pans and cool completely on a wire rack (**~45 minutes**).
3. Cakes must be completely cool before frosting.

### Step 8: Frost (Optional)
1. Beat softened butter until smooth.
2. Gradually add powdered sugar, one cup at a time.
3. Add heavy cream, vanilla, and salt.
4. Beat on high speed for **3 minutes** until light and fluffy.
5. Frost cooled cake layers as desired.

|---|---|
| Oven Temperature | 350°F / 175°C |
| Pan Size | 8-inch round (2 pans) |

| Doneness Test | Toothpick comes out clean |
| Storage | Room temperature, covered, up to 3 days |
| Freezing | Wrap tightly; freezes up to 3 months |

---

## Tips for Success
- **Room temperature ingredients** are essential for proper emulsification.
- **Do not overmix** after adding flour — this develops gluten and makes the cake tough.
- **Measure flour correctly**: spoon into measuring cup and level off, or use a kitchen scale.
- **Test oven temperature** with an oven thermometer — ovens can vary by ±25°F.
