# Classic Vanilla Cake

A moist, fluffy vanilla cake perfect for any occasion. This recipe yields one 9-inch round cake or two 8-inch layers.

---

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt

### Wet Ingredients
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- 1 3/4 cups granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 1 cup whole milk, room temperature

### Optional Frosting
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 3 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

---

## Equipment
- Two 9-inch round cake pans (or one 9x13 inch pan)
- Electric mixer (stand or hand)
- Mixing bowls (large and medium)
- Spatula
- Wire cooling rack
- Parchment paper

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Grease the cake pans with butter and line the bottoms with parchment paper.
3. Lightly flour the pans, tapping out any excess.

### Step 2: Mix Dry Ingredients
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
2. Set aside.

### Step 3: Cream Butter and Sugar
1. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar together on medium-high speed for 3–4 minutes, until light and fluffy.
2. Scrape down the sides of the bowl with a spatula.

### Step 4: Add Eggs and Vanilla
1. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
2. Mix in the vanilla extract.

### Step 5: Combine Wet and Dry
1. Reduce mixer speed to low.
2. Add the dry ingredients in three parts, alternating with the milk in two parts, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients.
3. Mix until just combined — do not overmix.

### Step 6: Bake
1. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans.
2. Smooth the tops with a spatula.
3. Bake for **30–35 minutes** at **350°F (175°C)**.
4. The cake is done when a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Step 7: Cool
1. Let the cakes cool in the pans for 10 minutes.
2. Remove from pans and transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.

### Step 8: Frost (Optional)
1. Beat the softened butter until creamy.
2. Gradually add powdered sugar, heavy cream, vanilla, and salt.
3. Beat on high speed for 2–3 minutes until fluffy.
4. Spread or pipe onto the cooled cake.

## Tips
- Ensure all ingredients are at **room temperature** for the best texture.
- Do not overmix the batter — this can result in a dense cake.
- For a richer flavor, substitute half the milk with buttermilk.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
